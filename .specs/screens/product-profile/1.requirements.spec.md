# Product Profile - Requirements

## Purpose

Present a product's commercial, inventory, promotion, and audit context with
clear navigation to the product list and editing flow.

## Responsive behavior

- At narrow widths, identifiers and copy actions remain within the available
  profile content width.
- Long identifiers truncate visually without changing the copied value.
- Copy controls remain at least 32 by 32 logical pixels and expose a tooltip.
- The profile must not produce RenderFlex overflow exceptions at narrow widths.

## Scenarios

- Given a product profile at 320 logical pixels, when SKU and barcode are
  rendered, then no layout exception occurs and copy actions remain visible.
- Given a long SKU or barcode, when copied, then the full original value is
  placed on the clipboard even when its label is ellipsized.
